national background and jobs requiring a current quote. No national range was
inflated mechanically or represented as a surveyed local price.

## Limits of plumbing and small-job research

No defensible complete-job local range was established for the generic small
plumbing repair card or every standard outlet replacement. Their headlines now
request a local quote. Older national numbers remain only as explicitly
limited background inside expandable notes.

Reviewed but not adopted as an all-in small-repair range:

- Angi's Philadelphia plumbing page, updated July 9, 2026, gives $183–$509
  across plumbing services. It is not a matching scope or a suburban quote.
  https://www.angi.com/articles/plumber-cost/pa/philadelphia
- Fishtown Home Solutions publishes faucet replacement $165–$350 **labor
  only** and toilet repair $150–$375. These narrow contractor examples do
  not establish all-in licensed-plumber prices for all repairs. Its live
  plumbing page does not state a publication date.
  https://philadelphiahandymanhomeservices.com/services/plumbing-repairs/

Before replacing quote-required headlines with numbers, obtain a complete
scope and current local price, including visit minimum, diagnosis, parts,
access, permits where applicable, and after-hours charges. Do not add a
percentage merely because it is 2026.
