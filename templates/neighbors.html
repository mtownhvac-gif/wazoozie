How the $25 service credit works · offer terms

What earns the $25 credit?

  • The referrer must be 18 or older. Refer a different household that has not previously paid Middletown HVAC for work. Self-referrals and referrals within your own household do not qualify.
  • Your friend must tell Middletown HVAC your name or provide your Wazoozie referral link before booking. Personal links must be reported within 90 days of creation. The office confirms your identity before awarding credit; a personal link is optional. If several people refer the same household, only the first referral recorded by the office qualifies.
  • The friend’s first Middletown HVAC service, maintenance or installation job must be completed and paid in full. An inquiry, estimate, free visit, canceled job or fully refunded job does not qualify.
  • The office checks the referral, new-customer status and paid job before recording one $25 credit per referred household. Saving a brief or seeing a referral receipt is not proof of eligibility, delivery, booking or earned credit.
  • Use an earned credit toward a future Middletown HVAC service, maintenance or installation invoice. One $25 referral credit per invoice; no cash value, cash payout, transfer or application to a previous invoice. Any unused portion stays as credit. Earned credits do not expire.
  • Your friend does not receive a discount under this offer. Service area, availability, fees and job pricing are confirmed directly with Middletown HVAC. No other listed business is committed to any reward.
  • When sharing, let your friend know you may receive a $25 service credit. Share personally; don’t post another person’s information, send unsolicited bulk messages or claim a recommendation is an independent review.

Keep your referral link. Contact Middletown HVAC through its business profile to ask about verification or use of an earned credit. Credit review and redemption are handled by the office, not an automatic Wazoozie rewards balance.

Offer terms effective November 8, 2026. Referrer contact details and saved briefs are retained by Wazoozie for about 90 days. Referrals reported to the office and earned credits are recorded separately in Middletown HVAC’s customer records.
