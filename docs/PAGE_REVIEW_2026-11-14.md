# Wazoozie page review — November 14, 2026

Reviewed all 83 sitemap pages, the account page and the 404 page: 85 distinct customer-facing routes. The duplicated 404 HTML entry was also rebuilt. The unlisted model-testing workbench remains outside public navigation and search discovery.

## What changed

- Applied one shared navigation, type, spacing, contrast and responsive layout pass across the customer-facing site. Kept the existing brand, direct contacts, forms and tool behavior.
- Replaced vague headline fragments with descriptive headings and reviewed the full page copy. Removed unsupported “vetted/trusted” marketing language. Authentic review excerpts, source links, price attribution, safety instructions, retention terms and recovery limits were preserved.
- Corrected privacy, standards, planner, Middletown profile and machine-readable delivery descriptions: enabled, consented home-service requests go through Wazoozie Help for review and forwarding. Direct calls and bookings remain direct. Email-service acceptance is not inbox receipt or a booking.
- Made the current Pennsylvania focus clear; New Jersey and Delaware coverage remains unconfirmed.
- Put business/contact paths before optional tools on the homepage. The complete family illustration now fits without cutting off heads. Optional tools, quizzes and referral details remain available.
- Added relevant next-step links and privacy-safe sharing. Shared links omit query strings and fragments; sharing does not submit a lead, save notes or send a message automatically. Automotive, wrap and real-estate pages use their appropriate contact categories.
- Added scan links to long pages and corrected misleading or vague metadata. Styles remain in one versioned bundle per page.

## Validation

- Production build and all 213 automated checks pass. Checks cover intake consent, recipient routing, retries, account boundaries, sources, internal links, structured data, CSS bundling and the new sharing behavior.
- Browser-rendered all 85 routes at widths of 1440, 390 and 320 pixels. Final checks found one heading per page, the correct route identity, one stylesheet and no horizontal page overflow at any tested width.
- Fixed small-screen overflow in the project finder and hidden-work notebook during the review. Verified the live finder’s named contact and automotive category routing locally.
- Reviewed desktop and mobile examples from the homepage, business profiles, service pages, guides, long-form information and request tools.
- No new personal data, marketing subscriptions, outbound customer messages, paid ads, account permissions or delivery integrations were introduced. No promise of traffic, rankings, inbox delivery or booked jobs is made.
