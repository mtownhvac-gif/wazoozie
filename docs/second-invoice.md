# The Second Invoice

An original Wazoozie project interaction: preview conditional rework caused by a proposed order of home projects, compare a suggested order, and take the assumptions and questions to the trades. No claim of worldwide novelty is made.

Public route: `/second-invoice/`. The homepage, project desk, tools page and footer link to it.

`assets/second-invoice-model.mjs` owns the eight project choices, conditional ordering rules, shared trade conversations, gates, topological ordering and allowlisted URL encoding. `assets/second-invoice.js` renders the browser experience. `second_invoice.py` adds the page during the existing production build.

No backend, model API, paid service, tracking identifier or new binding is required. Choices and self-reported discussion flags can be saved in browser storage. Shared URL fragments contain only a version, project IDs and assumption IDs. Discussion flags are excluded. No freeform information is collected. Clearing a browser plan cannot invalidate previously shared links.

The preview is a limited editorial planning model. It offers no dollar estimates, savings claims, diagnosis, code determinations or contractor approvals. The suggested order is a discussion aid; actual project phases may overlap. Separate-area work and access assumptions can turn ordering rules off. Site conditions and written scopes establish the actual responsibilities and sequence.

Validation covers all 2,081 ordered combinations of up to four of eight projects, conditional assumptions, unresolved gates, share-data allowlisting and the built UI's reorder, compare, share and clear actions. Existing site and backend checks remain in place.

Partner use: invite a business to review the questions for its trade, confirm its public contact information and link to the tool for customers planning related projects. Do not imply the business has endorsed the tool before it agrees. No referral fees or paid placements are introduced.
