# Wazoozie
Your home deserves good people.

## Concept
A premium local home-services referral network where homeowners
choose the business they want to contact.

## Initial services
- HVAC, boilers, water heaters, and tankless: Middletown HVAC
- Electrical: Ryan Dugan Electric
- General plumbing and drains: Liberator

Additional providers and service areas will be added after confirmation.

## Experience
Keep the warm, polished design. Let visitors choose their town,
select a service, browse businesses, and contact their chosen provider.
