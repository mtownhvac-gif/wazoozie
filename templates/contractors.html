BEFORE A PROFILE GOES LIVE

A useful introduction starts with clear information.

  1. Your business and contact details.

    A real business name and someone the homeowner can reach.

  2. Your work and coverage.

    The services you actually provide and the areas you currently serve.

  3. Relevant documents and sources.

    Current registration, licensing and insurance as applicable, plus permission to use submitted project photos or review sources.

  4. Clear participation terms.

    Scope, placement and any charges agreed before a paid arrangement starts.

Paid promotion does not establish credentials or guarantee the outcome of a job. Homeowners and businesses agree on work directly.
